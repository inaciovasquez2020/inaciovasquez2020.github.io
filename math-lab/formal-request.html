Prepare a verification request.

This beta creates a local, portable input package for future Lean kernel verification. It does not compile Lean, examine a proof, run an axiom audit, upload data, charge a fee, or issue a certificate.

01 / MANIFEST

Source and environment

Untrusted input warning

A malformed theorem, invented proof step, unpinned dependency, hidden assumption, changed file, or incomplete Lean environment prevents reliable certification. Any future verification must use an isolated trusted kernel, exact source and pinned dependencies, resource guards and independent replay. A hash is not a proof.

USER CONSENT — NOT A PROOF VERDICT

NOT_RUN — No proof has been checked; no certificate has been issued.

02 / TRUST AND PAYMENTS

Conditions for a future machine-verification certificate

A future service must execute an exact Lean toolchain, verify the target theorem at its actual use site, audit axioms for unapproved assumptions, reject admitted or opaque proof shortcuts, record hashes, and provide replayable signed provenance. Timeout and resource exhaustion mean INCOMPLETE, never PASS.

No payment system exists in this beta. A future paid service may charge for bounded computation or review, never for a guaranteed positive verdict. Deployment of such a service requires separate security, privacy, billing, and independent-verification review.
