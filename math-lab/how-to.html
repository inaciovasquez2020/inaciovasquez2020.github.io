How to use the Math Verification Lab.

The Lab offers two different operations. An exact polynomial check can certify a supported identity or give an exact counterexample. A manuscript audit reads LaTeX structure but does not check mathematical truth. Choose the method before interpreting any result.

01 / QUICK START

Four steps

  1. Open the verification workbench and choose a Verification method.
  2. Enter a Claim ID and Source reference (use Manual entry for an example). These are provenance labels, not independent confirmation.
  3. Paste your LaTeX or load a file. The Lab automatically suggests the subject field from keywords in manuscript mode. Review the field and subcategory; ambiguous input remains Other / Unclassified. You can override the suggestion. Field classification is not proof.
  4. Paste the equation or manuscript into the box, or select a local .tex/.txt file. Run the selected method and read the scoped result.

Field confirmation required: Automatically suggested categories are unverified. The user must inspect the displayed field/subcategory and check the confirmation box. FIELD MISSING / REVIEW REQUIRED appears for ambiguous or unclassified input; a specific category must be selected manually. Any manuscript edit or category change resets confirmation. Confirmation does not verify mathematics.

Equation extraction review: Math may be missed because of unusual delimiters, custom environments, split expressions, or transcription errors. This Lab flags selected unsupported display environments, unbalanced delimiters, inline math, and mismatched display blocks; it shows literal source excerpts, line numbers and reasons. Absence of flags is not evidence of complete extraction. Compare the listed equations with the original.

Optional walkthrough: From the Lab homepage, choose “Start illustrated walkthrough” to view five schematic steps. It is dismissible at any time and does not require an account or transmit your source. For AI-generated source, see the AI-assisted LaTeX preparation guide.

Input warning: Incorrect or incomplete transcription, incorrect assumptions, unsupported notation and mislabeled subject matter can invalidate the interpretation of a result. The Lab does not establish that entered text matches the author's intended claim. Every result states the actual engine and its scope, separately from the manuscript's subject.

Important: Algebra / Polynomial identities over R is the only exact-supported field and subcategory. For physics, topology, information theory, and other subjects, the manuscript structure audit remains available but cannot prove the claims.

02 / EXACT ALGEBRA

Verify an equation, not a whole paper

Choose Exact polynomial identity, with field Algebra and subcategory Polynomial identities over R. The engine handles integer coefficients, one variable x, powers 0–16, and syntactic degree at most 32. It accepts a limited LaTeX subset such as \left, \right, \cdot and braces in exponents. It does not support general division, calculus, multivariable equations, inequalities or arbitrary LaTeX macros.

Expected PASS

(x+1)^2 = x^2 + 2x + 1

Expected EXACT_CERTIFICATE_PASS. For the degree-2 bound, evaluations at 0, 1 and 2 establish an identity between these polynomials over the reals.

Expected refutation

(x+1)^2 = x^2 + 1

Expected REFUTED: at x = 1, the left side is 4 and the right side is 2.

On this mode, dropping a .tex document extracts candidate displayed equalities. Select one equation to evaluate. Unrecognized mathematics must be explicitly rejected as unsupported.

03 / MANUSCRIPT STRUCTURE AUDIT

Understand what the paper contains

Choose Manuscript structure audit, then select the most appropriate field and subcategory (for example, Mathematical physics / Spectral theory and operators for a normal-mode paper). Paste a LaTeX document or load a .tex or .txt file of at most 64 KiB.

On paste or upload, the field selector updates automatically if keywords suggest a subject. It is always labeled AUTO-SUGGESTED (UNCONFIRMED). A manually chosen field remains under your control. If the text spans multiple subjects or no match is found, the suggestion is Other / Unclassified and the method is still only structural.

The tool locates definition, lemma, theorem, corollary and proposition environments, counts proof blocks, inventories displayed mathematics and may provide rule-based review prompts. It is not an AI mathematical reviewer, a literature verifier, or a theorem prover.

Minimal manuscript example

\begin{lemma}[Illustrative claim]
For all real x, (x+1)^2=x^2+2x+1.
\end{lemma}

The manuscript audit extracts the lemma and marks it NOT_VERIFIED. To verify the polynomial equality, copy that equation separately into Exact polynomial identity.

04 / EVIDENCE STATUS

How to interpret the verification record

StatusMeaning
EXACT_CERTIFICATE_PASSSupported polynomial identity established by exact arithmetic and a degree-bound argument. Not a Lean proof.
REFUTEDExact witness where a claimed universal polynomial equality fails.
NOT_VERIFIEDStatement extracted or reviewed structurally; the Lab has not determined whether it is true or false.
STRUCTURAL_ONLYSelected method inventories manuscript text and mathematical environments, but cannot issue proof verdicts.
NO_ENGINENo exact verification engine is connected for that selected field/subcategory.
REGRESSION_CANDIDATEA proposed casebook test. It has not been independently reviewed or promoted.
INCOMPLETE_SOURCE_REQUIREDThe exported manuscript casebook preserves metadata but not enough source to independently verify a mathematical claim.

Example: Your chiral-vibration document returning 1 definition / 1 lemma / 1 proof block / 10 displayed equations and NOT_VERIFIED is a successful structure extraction test, not a mathematical proof.

05 / PRIVACY AND REPRODUCIBILITY

What is shared?

Processing happens locally in the browser. The page does not automatically upload manuscripts or equations, call an AI service, or save a session to browser storage. The current browser JavaScript is publicly inspectable under the website's MIT license; do not put private algorithms in website source code.

The optional Export review casebook JSON action saves a local file for independent review. The casebook may include entered equations and source references. A manuscript entry records limited structure metadata, rather than the full manuscript. Check exports before sharing.

Every supported exact check produces bounded metamorphic regression candidates. This does not automatically improve or retrain the checker. Permanent changes require independent replay, source review and approved tests. See the technical verification protocol for implementation limits.

Formal-verification boundary: a successful browser certificate, a passed CI workflow or an extracted theorem does not establish any unrelated theorem, including global claims in topology or mathematical physics.
