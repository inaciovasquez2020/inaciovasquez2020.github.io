GUIDE / AI-ASSISTED TRANSCRIPTION

Using AI to prepare LaTeX.

AI assistants can help format manuscripts but may change equations, omit hypotheses, invent steps or substitute notation. Always compare generated LaTeX with the authoritative source. AI agreement is not formal proof.

01 / HUMAN REVIEW

Before submitting AI-generated source

  1. Use only material you have permission to share with the selected AI provider.
  2. Request faithful transcription; preserve signs, indices, quantifiers, assumptions and equation order.
  3. Require an explicit UNCERTAIN marker for unreadable source, rather than invented content.
  4. Compare the output against the original document; record editorial changes.
  5. Select a method and confirm its subject field in the Lab. Review extraction warnings and evidence limits.
02 / COPYABLE INSTRUCTION

Prompt for an AI assistant

Transcribe this mathematical source into LaTeX
faithfully. Preserve all signs, notation, hypotheses,
quantifiers, equation order and theorem statements.
Do not repair, infer or invent proof steps.
Mark ambiguous or illegible source as UNCERTAIN
with its source location. List any editorial changes
separately. Do not claim mathematical verification.
I will compare the transcription to the original.

The Lab processes source locally, but sending manuscripts to an external AI service is a separate disclosure. Review that service's privacy terms before using unpublished research.

03 / WHAT RESULTS MEAN

Do not confuse parsing with proof

NOT_VERIFIED means the manuscript claim was identified, not proved or refuted. EXTRACTION REVIEW REQUIRED means certain displayed or inline mathematics may be missing; flagged excerpts must be compared against the original. Absence of flags does not guarantee completeness.

EXACT_CERTIFICATE_PASS applies only to supported univariate integer-polynomial equalities. The field-selection suggestion and a successfully compiled LaTeX manuscript are not proof certificates.
