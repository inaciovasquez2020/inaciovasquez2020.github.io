Melanoma CD36: matched baseline evidence

In a secondary single-cell RNA-seq analysis of 12 baseline melanoma patients (4 responders, 8 nonresponders), exact patient-level response-label permutation tests did not establish a statistically significant CD36–immunotherapy-response association.

Scope boundary: This is an exploratory computational record, not a clinically validated biomarker, treatment recommendation, mechanistic proof or independently replicated finding. Candidate myeloid clusters have not been definitively annotated as macrophages.

What was checked

The analysis matched malignant and tumor-microenvironment samples from the same 12 pretreatment patients. An exact cell-ID overlap audit identified 17 known malignant cells among 941 initial cluster-10/13 myeloid candidates, leaving 924 corrected primary candidates. An extended cluster-10/13/16 definition retained 1,137 candidates.

The original analysis averaged log1p(10,000 × CD36 count / RNA library size) over cells within each patient and tested the between-group mean difference by enumerating all 495 assignments of four responders among 12 patients.

A separate exploratory test used unlogged library-size-adjusted cell means and patient-level AUROC. For corrected primary myeloid CD36, response-high AUROC was 0.40625, with exact permutation p = 338/495. Because the statistics and transformations differ, these results must not be conflated.

Reproducibility and limitations

Both original mean-difference statistics were reproduced from source RDS objects. Six selected repository melanoma regression suites passed on the researcher's Mac before the evidence-only addendum commit. The public branch records aggregate summaries, source attribution, data hashes and methodological reconciliation, but does not redistribute the raw single-cell data or specimen-level records.

Definitive macrophage annotation, independent cohort validation, source-supplement authenticity assessment and clinical prediction remain open or not established. The frozen melanoma v2 predictor is unchanged; its external validation was not successful.
