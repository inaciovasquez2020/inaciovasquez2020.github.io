Independent global Poincaré derivation: OPEN. The universal strict net vertex-descent hypothesis G is equivalent to the classical Poincaré statement under Moise and Pachner connectivity. A conditional proof using G is not an independent solution.

The precise conditional theorem

For a finite connected closed simply connected combinatorial three-manifold K, define G to assert that whenever K has more than five vertices, some finite legal Pachner path leads to a triangulation with strictly fewer vertices. Under G, strong induction and the five-vertex classification give |K| homeomorphic to S3. Conversely, classical Poincaré recognition, Moise's PL uniqueness, and Pachner connectivity imply G. Thus G is a reformulation, not a weaker auxiliary lemma.

Exact move-count evidence

Define g2(K) = f1(K) − 4f0(K) + 10. Any normalization sequence to the boundary of the four-simplex satisfies N41−N14 = f0(K)−5 and N32−N23 = g2(K).

For joins of cycles Cm*Cn (m,n ≥ 4), a proven paper lower bound is (m−2)(n−2)+3 Pachner moves. The explicit C4*Cn construction attains the bound 2n−1.

Counterexamples and safeguards

A six-vertex stacked sphere refutes universal zero-cost removal of an arbitrary edge while retaining all original vertices and a protected star. An independent example shows that an induced suspended link cycle does not force the suspension edge to be absent elsewhere. These conditions must remain visible in any claimed local-to-global proof.
